Privacy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Coffee Battles has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no user database. This page lists the data that is still processed when you visit, and by whom.

This page is available in several languages; if versions differ, the English one applies.

Who this covers

This policy covers the website and game at coffeebattles.online. The project is maintained in the public COFFEE_BATTLES repository on GitHub. You can reach the project through the channels on the Contact page.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical data for each request, such as your IP address, browser and device details, the page requested and the time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it reliable and protect it from abuse.

Data stored in your browser

The site and the game save your chosen language in your browser’s local storage, so you do not have to pick it again. It never leaves your device. You can remove it by clearing this site’s data in your browser. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Google AdSense

The site includes Google AdSense code, currently for publisher verification; no ads are placed in the game. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure them, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or not.

Google explains how it uses data from partner sites on its partner sites page, and you can manage ad personalisation in Google Ads Settings.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires consent, advertising storage and processing will be offered through a consent tool certified for Google’s publisher requirements, where you can accept, reject or choose individual purposes and vendors.

Analytics

Coffee Battles does not run its own analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated when the tool is switched on, with its purpose and consent requirements.

Things you send us

If you contact the project through GitHub or another service, what you submit is handled under that service’s own terms. GitHub issues are public: never post passwords, payment details, health information or other sensitive data there.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, Google and other services take you off this site; their own privacy policies apply there.

Children

Coffee Battles is not aimed at children and does not build profiles of anyone. Advertising is configured to follow Google’s publisher policies, including limits on personalised ads where they apply.

Changes

This policy changes when the site adds or removes hosting, analytics, advertising, payment or account features. The date at the top shows the latest version.
