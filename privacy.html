Privacy policy

Privacy and data use

Last updated: 29 September 2026

COFFEE BATTLES is a small browser-based game project. The current version does not require an account and does not operate a custom user-profile database. This page explains the technical data that may still be processed by hosting and advertising services.

1. Site operator and scope

This policy applies to the COFFEE BATTLES website and browser game available at coffeebattles.online. The project is maintained through the public COFFEE_BATTLES repository on GitHub. Questions that do not contain sensitive personal information can be sent through the project contact channels listed on the Contact page.

2. Information you provide

The game itself currently has no registration form, account system, newsletter form or in-game profile. If you voluntarily contact the project through GitHub or another external service, the information you choose to submit is processed according to that service's own terms and privacy policy. Please do not post passwords, payment information, health information or other sensitive personal data in a public issue.

3. Technical and hosting data

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with normal web hosting, requests to the site may result in technical information being processed by the hosting provider, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, timestamps and security or diagnostic data. This processing is used to deliver the site, maintain reliability and protect the service from abuse.

4. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

The site includes Google AdSense integration code for publisher verification and may display Google-served advertising after approval. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and measure advertising activity. Google's use of advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Depending on region, consent choices and advertising configuration, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Users can learn about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use Google services at Google's partner-sites information page, and can manage advertising personalisation through Google Ads Settings.

5. Consent in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where required, advertising-related storage and processing will be presented through a consent management platform that supports the applicable Google publisher requirements. Users can accept, reject or manage the available purposes and vendors through that consent interface. The project intends to use Google's certified consent-management tooling rather than a custom cookie banner for Google advertising.

6. Cookies and local storage

COFFEE BATTLES itself currently does not require a user account cookie. Third-party services used by the site, including Google advertising services and the hosting platform, may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses or similar identifiers for security, consent, measurement and advertising purposes. The exact set of technologies can vary according to user region, consent status and service configuration.

7. Analytics

No separate first-party analytics database is currently operated by COFFEE BATTLES. If additional analytics tools are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before or when those tools are activated, including the relevant purposes and consent requirements.

8. External links

The site links to external services such as GitHub and Google. Opening those links leaves COFFEE BATTLES, and the destination service's privacy policy applies to activity on that service.

9. Children

COFFEE BATTLES is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly operate a system for creating profiles of children. Advertising configuration is intended to follow the applicable Google publisher policies, including restrictions on personalised advertising where required.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the project adds or removes hosting, analytics, advertising, payment or account features. The date at the top of the page will be updated when material changes are published.

Current advertising status: AdSense verification code is present, but the project is not yet placing visible ad units in the active battle experience. Advertising placement and consent controls will be configured separately before normal ad serving.
