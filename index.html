How a battle goes

  1. Set up

    Choose army size and terrain, and whether you attack or defend.

  2. Deploy

    Drag infantry, spearmen, archers and cavalry into your zone. The enemy deploys out of sight.

  3. Fight

    Give orders in real time. Facing, charges, fatigue and morale decide who breaks first.
