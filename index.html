What makes it different

A small battlefield with systems that matter

Formation and facing

Units are not abstract points. Their front, flank and rear matter, and formation changes alter how a regiment occupies space and brings its fighting strength to bear.

Energy and morale

Movement and combat consume energy. Exhausted troops lose effectiveness, while morale pressure can turn a damaged regiment into a retreating one even before every soldier is gone.

Terrain and movement

Roads, forests, walls, water and elevation shape routes and lines of sight. Positioning is intended to create tactical choices rather than simply decorate the field.

Current build

An evolving public battle prototype

The current build focuses on the core combat loop: deployment, orders, charges, retreats, ranged attacks, pursuit, terrain interaction and battlefield victory. New unit types and AI behaviour are being developed incrementally, while the game remains deliberately compact enough to load and test quickly.

Infantry

The baseline for line fighting: steady movement, formation control and direct melee engagement.

Cavalry

Fast movement and high-impact charges reward timing, open routes and attacks against exposed flanks or rears.

Archers

Ranged units can engage from distance, move into range and support the battle without being forced into melee unless ordered aggressively.

Design goal

Fast to start, deep enough to study

COFFEE BATTLES is both a playable experiment and a systems laboratory. Battles are intentionally quick to restart so changes to movement, pathfinding, morale, targeting and unit behaviour can be observed repeatedly. The project is published openly while those systems are refined.
