How to play

A battle has three steps: set up, deploy, fight. This page covers each one, then the rules that decide who wins.

1. Set up

Army size is the budget in points each side can spend, from 500 to 2000 or a custom amount. Terrain picks the kind of field; Random chooses one for you.

Armies decides who builds what. In Auto the computer builds the enemy army and you build yours. In Mirror the enemy copies your army. In Custom you build both.

Your role sets the win condition. As the attacker you must win before time runs out; as the defender you win by holding out until it does. Random draws the role when the battle starts.

2. Deploy

Drag regiments from the cards onto the field and drop them inside your deployment zone. Each card shows what the regiment is good at and what it costs. Drag a placed regiment to move it, turn it in 15° steps, or remove it. In Auto mode the enemy is already deployed and stays hidden until the battle starts.

3. Controls

Select
Left click a regiment. Ctrl + click adds or removes one; hold the right button and drag to draw a box.
Move
Right click the ground. Shift + right click adds a waypoint to the route.
Attack
Right click an enemy. Shift + right click an enemy makes it the attack at the end of the route.
Camera
Mouse wheel to zoom, drag with the middle button to pan.
Speed
The buttons at the top pause the battle or run it at 1×, 2× or 3×.
Space
Pauses and shows the game’s full HTML source. Space again closes it.
Touch
Tap to select, move or attack. Long press to add to the selection or add a waypoint. One finger dragged from the ground draws a box; two fingers pan and zoom.

4. Orders

The panel in the bottom left gives orders to the selected regiments. Distances are in field units (U); each grid square is 1U.

Stance

Hold waits for your orders. Aggressive attacks enemies within 10U and charges within 5U. Elusive keeps away, falling back from enemies within 9U. A stance never cancels an order you gave by hand.

Formation

Base is 3U wide and 2U deep, Column 2U wide and 3U deep, Line 6U wide and 1U deep. A wider front brings more soldiers into contact. Changing formation takes 2 seconds and is allowed during combat.

Charge, retreat, fire

Charge switches the regiment to charge speed until it hits; the impact adds shock to the target, and cavalry doubles it. Retreat breaks contact and pulls back at least 11U from the enemy. Fire at will lets archers, crossbowmen, light infantry and light cavalry shoot automatically at anything in range. A target you pick yourself is shot at even with fire at will off.

5. What decides a fight

Facing. A regiment attacked in the flank fights worse, and attacks on the flank or rear add much more shock than a frontal clash.

Energy. Marching and fighting use energy; standing still recovers it. With no energy left a regiment cannot move and stops fighting effectively.

Morale. Losses and shock wear morale down from steady to shaken, wavering and finally rout, when the regiment flees on its own. A routed regiment that gets away from the enemy can rally. One that crosses the dashed edge of the field will not rally again.

6. Terrain and sight

Rivers can only be crossed at bridges and fords. Streams are rough ground. Mountains and buildings are solid obstacles, and cavalry cannot enter forests. Your regiments see about 20U around them; enemies out of sight disappear, and their last known position stays marked.

7. Winning

The bar at the top of the field shows each side’s progress. When a side reaches 100%, because half of the enemy army’s points have been destroyed or have fled, the whole enemy army breaks and runs. The battle ends when one army has no regiments left on the field.

If ten minutes of game time pass first, the defender wins. At 2× or 3× speed that takes less in real time.
