About Coffee Battles

A real-time tactical battle game that runs in the browser. Two armies, one field, ten minutes at most. No account and nothing to install.

What you do

You choose the size of the armies and the terrain, deploy your regiments, and then command them while the battle runs. The enemy army is led by the computer, or by you if you want to set up both sides.

What decides a battle

Regiments are blocks of soldiers with a front, two flanks and a rear. Hitting a flank or the rear hurts far more than meeting the enemy head-on, and turning a regiment takes time. Every regiment tires as it marches and fights, and its morale drops under losses and shock until it breaks and runs. Rivers, forests, hills and villages shape where you can go, and you only see the enemy your troops can see.

There are eight kinds of regiment: infantry, light infantry, heavy infantry, spearmen, archers, crossbowmen, cavalry and light cavalry.

Where it stands

Coffee Battles is a public prototype. Units, balance and the interface change from one version to the next, and every change is visible in the GitHub repository.

The name

A battle should fit in a coffee break.
